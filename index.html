The problem

The invoice is a bad alerting tool.

Bill shock

A retry loop or a runaway agent can burn 5x your normal spend overnight. Most people find out 30 days later, when the invoice lands.

Split spend

Every provider has its own usage page, and each one shows yesterday's totals. Nobody has the one number that matters right now.

Dashboards don't page you

Observability tools are built for debugging sessions you have to open. Spikewatch only speaks when something is wrong.

How it works

Set it up once. Hear from it rarely.

  1. Connect a read-only keyIt can see usage numbers. It cannot make requests or change anything.
  2. It learns your normalEach hour is compared with your trailing average for that time of day.
  3. You get one messageOnly when spend crosses the line you set. Default is 3x.

The full mechanics - baselines, thresholds, the floor, the cooldown, and a worked example with real numbers - are on the product page.

Who it is for

  • Small teams running agents or batch jobs that call AI APIs on their own.
  • Founders who pay the bill personally and look at it once a month.
  • Anyone who found out a loop ran all night from the invoice.

What it is not

  • Not a hard cap. It never switches your service off.
  • Not a tracing tool. No prompts or responses are read or stored.
  • Not a dashboard to check. You hear from it only when something is wrong.
