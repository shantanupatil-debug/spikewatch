The math

What one missed weekend costs.

Pricing questions

Fair things to ask.

Why $15 and not free?

Because "free" monitoring gets ignored and abandoned. A small price keeps the hourly job maintained, the tests running, and the integrations coming - and it is still half of what the nearest paid alternative charges.

Why not free with a paid tier later?

The self-hosted engine already is the free tier, forever: same detection, your own repo, your own GitHub Actions minutes. The $15 buys not having to run or think about any of that.

What happens to the founding price after launch?

Teams that reserve during the founding period keep $15/month while it's founding. The public price later may be higher; yours isn't.

Is there a trial?

Better: the live checker is free forever and needs no signup. Run your last two weeks of real usage through the exact production detection and see what it would have sent. If it finds nothing worth alerting on, that answer was free too.

What if I need it for more than one provider?

Anthropic works today. Other providers are added only after their usage data access is confirmed - the waitlist email is how you'll hear when yours lands.
