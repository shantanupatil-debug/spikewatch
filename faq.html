Keys & data

Is my API key safe?

You never hand over your regular key. You create a read-only Anthropic Admin API key scoped to usage and cost reports - it can read usage numbers and nothing else. It cannot make requests, change settings, or see prompts and responses. Keys are stored encrypted, the detection code that uses them is open source, and revoking the key in the Anthropic Console stops everything in seconds.

Can Spikewatch see my prompts or my customers' data?

No, and not because of a policy - because of architecture. Spikewatch only reads the provider's usage report API, which contains token counts and cost data per model per hour. Prompts and responses are not in that API, and since there is no SDK or proxy, your traffic never passes through Spikewatch at all.

What happens to my usage data?

The hourly job reads the last 15 days of usage, computes, and moves on. There is no dashboard storing your history. Self-hosted, the whole thing runs inside your own GitHub repo with your own secrets.

Does the live checker upload anything?

No. The checker imports the same open-source engine into your browser and runs it there. Your pasted data never leaves the page - verifiable in your browser's network inspector. The optional Slack webhook test goes directly from your browser to Slack and is not stored.

Detection

How does it decide what "normal" is?

By default, the median of the same hour of day over your last 14 days - so Tuesday 3pm is judged against other 3pms, and weekends don't dilute weekdays. If your usage has no daily rhythm, a trailing-24-hour median baseline is one setting away. Medians are used so one big day doesn't silently raise the bar.

What are the threshold and the floor?

The threshold (default 3x) is how far above normal an hour must be. The floor (default $5/hour) is an absolute minimum, so 3x of a $0.40 hour stays quiet. Both are tunable; the full reference is in the docs.

Will it spam me during a long incident?

No. After an alert fires, repeats are suppressed for the cooldown window (default 6 hours) and counted. You get the first message, not a per-hour reminder of a fire you're already fighting.

Why hourly and not real time?

Provider usage APIs update on a delay and rate-limit reads, so hourly is the fastest honest cadence. A loop that starts at 2am is caught by 3am, not on the 30-day invoice. Services claiming instant spend alerts are reading the same delayed data.

What if I don't have 14 days of history yet?

The engine waits for enough matching hours to form a baseline (3 for same-hour mode, 12 for trailing-24) and stays silent until then. It would rather say nothing than guess.

Coverage & setup

Which providers does it watch?

Anthropic first - usage and cost reports through the Admin API are confirmed working, and the live checker already runs on Anthropic usage reports today. OpenAI, Google and OpenRouter are added only after their usage data access is confirmed end to end.

Do I have to change my code or add a dependency?

No. There is no SDK, no proxy, and nothing in your request path. If you can create a read-only usage key and a Slack webhook, the integration is done.

Will it cap or switch off my spend?

No. Spikewatch warns a human in time to act; it never touches your service. If you want hard caps, set them at your provider - Spikewatch makes sure you find out before it comes to that.

Can I self-host instead of paying?

Yes. The detection engine, the hourly job, and the GitHub Actions workflow are in the repo, and the self-host guide takes it step by step: two secrets and one enabled workflow. Free, forever, same detection.

Billing & the company

What does the $15 founding price include?

Hourly checks, Slack and email alerts, both baseline modes, every detection parameter, cancel any time. Nobody is charged before the first integration is live, and reserving needs only an email - full pricing details.

When do I actually pay?

Not when you join the waitlist - no card is taken. Billing starts only when the hosted service launches and your first integration is live.
